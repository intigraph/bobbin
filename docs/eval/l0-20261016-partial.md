## Coverage and failures

Retained 330 score rows plus one manifest: ten tasks × 11 arms × 3 budgets. That is 25% of planned task coverage, not a representative sample: `cargo-001`–`cargo-005` and `django-001`–`django-005` ran first; Go/nushell/pandas/polars/ruff/typst have no score rows. Outcomes: 213 injected, 83 skipped, 34 error. All are retained in [the exact safe scalar projection](l0-20261016-rows.csv); null metrics stay blank, never invented as success.

All 34 errors say `injected text had no parseable chunk headers`; they are invalid measurements, not zero retrieval quality. Of 83 skips, 42 report operational intent, 23 report previously-injected chunks and 18 report the semantic gate. The preregistration calls for `--no-dedup`; the 23 dedup-worded outcomes require contract reconciliation before another campaign. Their text alone does not establish a root cause. Neither skips nor errors are promoted to valid efficacy observations.
